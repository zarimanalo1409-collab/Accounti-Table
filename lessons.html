BASIC ACCOUNTING

The Accounting Equation

Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity

🟦 Assets

Assets are resources owned or controlled by a business.

Examples:

Cash, Land, Building, Equipment, Accounts Receivable

🟪 Liabilities

Liabilities are amounts owed by the business.

Examples:

Accounts Payable, Notes Payable, Loan Payable

🟩 Owner's Equity

Owner's equity represents the owner's claim on the business assets.

Examples:

Capital and Withdrawals


💡 Example

The owner invests ₱10,000 cash into the business.

The business now has ₱10,000 in Assets and ₱10,000 in Owner's Equity.

Therefore:

₱10,000 = ₱0 + ₱10,000

🧠 Remember

Every business transaction affects the accounting equation.

The equation must always remain balanced.
